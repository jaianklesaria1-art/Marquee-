Privacy

This page explains what Marquee, a maker of uPVC architectural fins and louvers based in Mumbai, does with information from this website. The short version: we only get what you choose to send us, we use it to reply to you, and we don't track you.

What you send us

When you use the enquiry form, we receive:

  • your name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • your company
  • the type of enquiry
  • anything you write in the message box

We use this only to answer your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange the work with you. We don't sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

The form is handled by Formspree, a form service that forwards your enquiry to our inbox. Formspree stores the submission on its servers so it can deliver it.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry only as long as we need it to reply and, if you book with us, to manage the booking. After that we delete it.

No tracking or analytics

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. We don't know who visits or what you look at.

Other services this site uses

A few parts of the site load from other companies. Each one only receives the technical details any website gets when your browser asks it for a file, such as your IP address and browser type.

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces. Your browser asks Google's servers for the font files, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • ImageKit hosts the site's photos and video. It sees your IP address when your browser loads an image.
  • Unsplash hosts some of the stock photos on the site. It sees your IP address when your browser loads an image.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just get in touch using the details below and we'll sort it out.
